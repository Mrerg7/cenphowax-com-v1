A Phoenix summer is a different aftercare problem than the rest of the country. UV that burns skin in fifteen minutes, pool water that dries follicles, sweat under waistbands, and a calendar full of weekend trips — each one interacts with freshly waxed skin. Here is how to get through it.

## Time your sessions against the heat

**Book mornings or evenings.** A mid-day appointment means walking out into 110° in the exact window your skin is most reactive. Morning slots give you the cool part of the day for the first few hours, which is when skin does its settling.

**Schedule 48 hours of shade.** Freshly waxed skin needs two days without direct sun — that includes the car-to-door dash across a parking lot. Plan sessions for days you will be indoors, or cover the area.

**Count backwards from events.** A three-to-five-day lead time covers initial redness while landing you smooth for the event itself. For a Friday wedding, wax the Monday or Tuesday before.

## Pools, spas, and the 48-hour rule

Chlorine and freshly opened follicles are a bad pairing: the first 48 hours stay out of pools, hot tubs, and open water. After that window:

- Rinse with fresh water right after swimming
- Moisturize the same evening — chlorine and desert air combine into a drying double act
- Treat any itchy, flaky patch as a signal to exfoliate sooner (gently) rather than scratch

The same rule covers the gym: heavy sweating is fine after 24 hours, but skip it in the first day when friction and salt are unwelcome on open follicles.

## Clothing is aftercare

Waistbands, seams, and synthetic fabric are the most under-discussed irritants after a [Brazilian](/services/brazilian-wax/). In summer heat:

- Loose shorts or a flowy dress home from the appointment
- Breathable fabric for the first 24–48 hours
- Skip anything that sits directly on the waxed area until redness settles

## Ingrown prevention in dry heat

Desert air pulls moisture out of skin, and dry skin is what traps regrowing hairs. The summer routine:

1. **Moisturize daily** — non-fragranced is fine; consistency beats product choice
2. **Exfoliate from day three**, two to three times a week ([full routine](/aftercare/))
3. **SPF 30+** on exposed waxed areas after the initial 48 hours
4. **Rebook at three weeks** — summer regrowth feels faster, and staying ahead of it prevents the length that causes ingrowns

## Adjust expectations, not standards

Results still last three to four weeks in summer — but heat, friction, and swimming make weeks three and four *feel* shorter. Shortening your interval by a week in summer is a schedule adjustment, not a failure.
