The hardest part of a first Brazilian wax is not the waxing — it is not knowing what is about to happen. Here is the entire appointment, start to finish, so you walk in knowing every step.

## Before you leave home

- **Hair length:** quarter to half inch (about two to three weeks of growth). Too short and the wax cannot grip; too long and we trim before starting.
- **Skin prep:** exfoliate the day before. No retinoids, acids, or self-tanner for 48 hours.
- **Clean skin:** shower beforehand, skip lotion and oil on the area.
- **Clothing:** wear something loose to change back into — snug waistbands feel unnecessary afterward.

Eat a light meal beforehand. Waxing on an empty stomach is the one avoidable discomfort of the day.

## In the room: minute by minute

**1. Consultation (3–5 minutes)**
We talk through how much hair you want removed — full Brazilian with an optional front strip, or complete removal — plus any medications, sensitivities, or recent treatments. This is the moment to mention everything: retinoids, antibiotics, recent sunburn, pregnancy, skin conditions.

**2. Prep and trim (5 minutes)**
The area is cleansed and, if needed, trimmed to length. Nothing is waxed yet.

**3. The first pull (the one you are listening for)**
Small section of warm hard wax goes on, sets for a few seconds, and lifts in one motion with the growth. It is quick and sharp — most clients describe the first pull as the loudest moment, mentally, of the whole appointment. It is over in under a second.

**4. Working through sections (25–35 minutes)**
We move in small sections — front, sides, between, back — following growth direction. Hard wax is applied and lifted section by section, never re-pulling the same skin twice. Sensitive spots get smaller sections and slower pacing; say "pause" whenever you want and we pause.

**5. Finishing (5 minutes)**
Soothing oil, a quick check, and aftercare guidance. Total appointment: 45–60 minutes.

## What it feels like

Sensation is sharpest on the first pull of each new area and eases as the session continues — your nervous system stops treating each pull as news. By the final sections most clients are conversational. Redness that settles within an hour or two is normal; see [aftercare](/aftercare/) if anything persists beyond that.

## The 48 hours afterward

- No pools, hot tubs, or open water for 48 hours
- No heavy workouts or tight clothing for 24 hours
- No direct sun on the area for 48 hours, then SPF
- Start gentle exfoliation on day three to keep ingrowns away

Full routine: [the aftercare guide](/aftercare/).

## When to come back

Three to four weeks. Not because four weeks is magic, but because hair growing past half an inch becomes harder to remove cleanly — and each session after that gets easier as regrowth returns finer.
